HOW TO READ THE REDUCTION COLUMN
------------------------------------------------------------
The headline ratio (up to 2.9e9x at n=33) is real but it is not a
function of n. Only 3 distinct sink states exist in this workload --
baseline, anchor-only, and anchor-plus-the-faulted-aggregate -- because
every other operator is state-insensitive and its flip is caught by the
output hash before any model is fit. The cost of the method therefore
tracks |F|, the active frontier, and not the size of the DAG.

That is the claim worth making, and it is the one to stress-test next:
the reduction collapses when a change touches many operators at once
(a shared library upgrade, a global config change, a backfill). Those
cases are absent from this sweep and belong in the evaluation.
